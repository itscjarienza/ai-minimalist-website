Common Questions

What people ask before they start.

  1. 01

    Do I need the diagnostic before the audit?

    Not required, but strongly recommended. The Diagnostic takes 20 minutes and makes the audit sessions faster and more focused. If you haven't done it, we'll run through the basics together in session one.

    // it's free — just do it

  2. 02

    What size business is this for?

    The audit is built for small business owners and operators running 5 to 50 people. Lean enough that one bad AI placement hurts. Operational enough that there's something to map.

    // if you're a solo founder just starting out, the Diagnostic is your best first step

  3. 03

    How long does the audit take?

    Two to three sessions of 60 to 90 minutes each. Most clients finish in two weeks from first session to final deliverable.

    // timeline depends on how complex your core workflows are

  4. 04

    What do I get at the end?

    A documented system map, a list of waste points, a prioritized AI placement plan, and a clear list of what to cut. Not a report — a working document you can act on immediately.

    // most clients implement their first change within 48 hours of the final session
