The Method

Map the system first.
Then decide where AI belongs.

Step 01

Map the System

Draw the process as it actually exists — not how you think it works.

Step 02

Find the Waste

Where is time lost to repetitive, predictable, pattern-driven tasks?

Step 03

Apply the Filter

"Could I train someone to handle this and hand me back exactly what I need?"

Step 04

Place the AI

Only where it passed the filter. One process. One fix. Not everywhere.

Step 05

Validate

Did the system get cleaner or just different? If not simpler — remove it.
